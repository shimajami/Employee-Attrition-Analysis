# Employee-Attrition-Analysis
## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition data to identify workforce trends associated with employee turnover. I conducted the analysis using SQL to clean, explore, and analyze the employee data. I then created an interactive Tableau dashboard to visualize the impact of attrition by department, job role, overtime, tenure, monthly income, and job satisfaction. 

The dashboard also includes a high-risk employee segmentation to identify combinations of workforce characteristics associated with high attrition rates. 

## Dashboard 
![Employee Attrition Dashboard](Dashboard%201.png)
The Tableau dashboard is interactive, which allows for users to select categories within the visualizations and filter the dashboard to explore specific employee groups.  

## Key findings
- The overall employee attrition rate is 16.12%.
- Employees working overtime have an attrition rate of 30.53%, compared with 10.44% for employees who do not work overtime.
- Sales Representatives have the highest attrition rate among job roles at 39.76%.
- Employees with 0–2 years of tenure have the highest tenure-based attrition rate at 29.82%.
- Employees earning under $3,000 per month have an attrition rate of 28.61%, substantially higher than the other income groups.
- Employees reporting low job satisfaction have the highest satisfaction-based attrition rate at 22.84%.
- The highest-risk segment identified in the analysis has an attrition rate of 60.87%.
